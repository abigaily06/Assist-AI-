## What does it do?
Our scheduling website would allow students to put their tasks into the chatbot and it would generate a personalised study schedule working around their workload and life we hope the user feels studying does not have to be overwhelming yet manageable whilst being on track to their goal
 Features for our website include:
Incorporating study tips and recommendations , a timer/focus mode, task tracking and prioritization, testing the user with study materials and summarising notes, calendar view mode, progress tracking
Our chatbot would identify stress patterns and analyse the users workload to prioritise tasks in able to make the schedule so essentially the ‘data’ is based onto what the user have said during the conversation
